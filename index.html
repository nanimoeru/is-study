Do you really understand what you think you understand?

Would you like to take part in our study, and help us understand together how Deaf people make best sense of a presentation in International Sign? Then write to us by 11 October. Wherever you are in the world.

What taking part involves

  • Who

    Deaf people, 18 or older, who have been to at least one international Deaf event where International Sign was used.

  • When

    Two online group sessions, about two hours each, roughly two weeks apart.

  • What

    You watch short extracts of real conference presentations in International Sign, and then we discuss them together.

  • Cost

    Nothing. Taking part is free and voluntary, and you can stop at any time.
